Prism Shift – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 07 October 2026

Prism Shift (“the app”) is a puzzle game made by VibrantGames (“we”). We built it to be safe for players of every age, including children. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: we don't collect any.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet, and it doesn't ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or photos.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves a small file on your own device. It holds your level stars, your energy, your bottle skin choice, your settings (sound, music, vibration, colour-blind symbols) and the days you solved the daily puzzle. This file never leaves your device and we can't see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and you can also clear it any time with “Reset progress” on the home screen.

Children's privacy

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live). Because the app collects no personal information, there is nothing to review or delete. Parents with questions can contact us using the details below.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics services. App stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) may collect information when you download an app. Their own privacy policies cover that.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example because a future version adds an optional feature, we'll update this page and change the date above before that version is released.
